Degrees, timing & funding

MSc and PhD inquiries are welcome. An MSc-to-PhD transfer may be possible after about a year, depending on progress and program requirements. Contact me about fit, timing, and available funding.

UVic admission requirements
Mentoring & research environment

Regular individual meetings, clear expectations for feedback and authorship, and training shaped around your project. Our wider Earth History community includes Anne-Sofie Ahm’s and Jon Husson’s groups.

Backgrounds & ways to participate

You need not arrive with every skill. Geology, chemistry, physics, mathematics, and computing can all contribute. We welcome different backgrounds and can discuss alternatives when field travel is a barrier.

Undergraduates and prospective postdocs are also welcome to inquire.
