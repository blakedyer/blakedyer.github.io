What We Do

Field observations become tests of ancient climate and sea level.

We combine field geology, geochemistry, and models to understand how sediments preserve and distort records of environmental change.

01 Reconstruct past shorelines. Connected reefs, beaches, and dunes reveal past sea levels and ice-sheet change. 02 Read sedimentary archives. Sediment movement and the formation of rock shape the history we can recover. 03 Test geochemical records. Chemical records help us trace the carbon cycle and test when geological clocks are reliable. 04 Build quantitative methods. Statistics, machine learning, and landscape models connect evidence to hypotheses.
