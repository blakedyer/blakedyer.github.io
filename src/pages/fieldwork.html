Cook Islands, 2026.

Living reefs and raised limestone offer complementary views of coastal change. In May and June 2026, the HiSEAS field team worked across Rarotonga, Atiu, Mangaia, Mauke, and Mitiaro.

The expedition report brings together field observations, maps, photographs, and the questions they raise. It was prepared for island leaders, schools, communities, and project partners.
