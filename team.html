People reading Earth’s history.

Our group connects field geology, geochemistry, and quantitative approaches through independent projects and shared questions.

Students lead their research with mentorship and support from Blake Dyer. We exchange ideas, data, and skills across the wider Earth History community at UVic.

Current members.

Blake Dyer

Principal investigator

Blake combines field geology, carbonate geochemistry, geospatial observations, and modeling to investigate how sediments record environmental change. His research connects sea-level history, the carbon cycle, and the interpretation of sedimentary archives.

Alumni and past members.

The questions, methods, and ideas developed here continue through the people who lead them.

Stacey Edmonsond

PhD · 2026

Now a McDonnell Center for the Space Sciences Postdoctoral Fellow at Washington University in St. Louis.

Stacey investigated how sedimentary and geochemical records reveal changes in Earth’s environment and carbon cycle. She led the development, testing, and documentation of StratMC, and used Bayesian inference to reconstruct the Lomagundi–Jatuli carbon-isotope excursion.

WebsiteStratMCGitHub

Miranda Reid

MSc · 2026

Now a PhD candidate at the University of Waterloo.

Miranda combined satellite imagery and machine learning to measure and predict coastal change in the Canadian Maritimes. Blake’s co-supervision focused on the machine-learning component of her research.
