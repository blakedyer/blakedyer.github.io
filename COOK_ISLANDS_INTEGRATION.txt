Cook Islands research integration

Purpose and editing
The research homepage features the 2026 campaign immediately after the landing
overview, with a hero shortcut. Field Photos includes a compact entry before
its existing albums. Both lead to cook-islands.html, a concise research summary
that connects field observation, dating, elevation and geodynamic interpretation.
Styling uses the existing theme.css tokens, with scoped campaign-* selectors.
The new page is included in sitemap.xml. Its canonical URL will be
https://blakedyer.github.io/cook-islands.html after deployment.

Canonical record
https://blakedyer.github.io/cook_islands_2026/ owns the evolving field record.
The new research page links to its Field Report, Sites and Tidal Datums routes;
it does not copy datasets or embed tour systems. It also connects HISEAS and
the existing sea-level publication section. Initial observations are explicitly
distinguished from a final sea-level history. Do not add ages or numerical
sea-level conclusions until the canonical report and analyses support them.
Image provenance: assets/images/cook-islands/SOURCES.json. Both accepted campaign
images are copied without altering their pixels; descriptive captions are kept.
