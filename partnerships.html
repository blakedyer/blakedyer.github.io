Help make Earth’s history more useful.

We welcome conversations with researchers, nonprofit organizations, educators, and people interested in supporting our work.

Our research asks how rocks record environmental change. Better interpretations of ancient coastlines and sedimentary archives strengthen the evidence available for understanding climate, sea level, and ice-sheet behavior.

Evidence that connects places and people.

Past warm periods offer a longer perspective on ice-sheet and sea-level change than the instrumental record alone. Interpreting their geological evidence requires field observations, reliable timelines, and models that account for the movement of land and water.

Our contribution is the underlying science and the methods needed to test it. This knowledge can inform the wider research used to understand long-term coastal change.
